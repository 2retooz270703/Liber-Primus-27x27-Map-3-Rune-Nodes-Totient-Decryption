## 1. Deriving the opening route from geometry

### Source landmarks

The same pair of black crosses marks all three pages in the original scans 17–19. P2 ends with a cicada illustration and a red marker made of 13 dots. The archive lists eight comparable spans from the beginning of a red heading to the next red 13-dot marker. Their lengths in runes are **729, 119, 20, 1021, 1433, 91, 308, 85**. Only the first length is a perfect square. For p0–p2, the count is **262 + 266 + 201 = 729 = 27²**. The original lines contain 19–25 runes, so arranging the stream in rows of 27 remains an interpretation. [Red-heading analysis](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/blob/d9789b9ce39bf9efe9d16ecfd2ac49842c6b1164/im-results/lp2-p0-red-heading-sequendi-ratio-route-check.md) · [Audit #1961](https://github.com/FlareZ123/liber-primus/issues/1961#issuecomment-7037673524)

Coordinates start at 1 and are written as **(row, column)**. The absolute position is `27(r−1)+c`. The map center is **(14,14)**, the last rune on p0 is **(10,19)**, the first rune on p1 is **(10,20)**, and the first rune on p2 is **(20,16)**. Rune indices 0–28 are used for subtraction modulo 29. Gematria Primus prime weights are used separately for checksums.

### Block 1. The endpoint determines the length of the segment

1. **Find the endpoint first.** Scan horizontal triplets of adjacent runes with matching outer runes. Look for one where moving φ(the middle rune’s index) cells to the right from the leftmost rune lands exactly at the center of the map. Only one triplet on the grid qualifies, **X–OE–X** at **(14,4–6)**. The index of OE is 22, and φ(22)=10. Moving 10 cells from **(14,4)** lands at **(14,14)**. The leftmost X both ends block 1 and starts the key node for block 2.
2. **Find the start.** The 13 runes in the red heading and φ(13)=12 give two coordinate orders, **(13,12)** and **(12,13)**. Four axes through each point give **8** three-rune nodes made of adjacent cells. The archive applies the same move from the project to each node. From the first outer cell, move in the positive direction along the axis by `φ(b)+φ(φ(b))`, where b is the index of the original middle rune. The start must lie on the map and occur no later in reading order than the fixed X at **(14,4)**. One candidate remains, the horizontal **AE–J–EA** at **(13,11–13)**.
3. **Decrypt the seven selected cells.** J=11 becomes I=10, and the **AE–I–EA** key has phase 0. The displacement is **10+4=14**, giving a start at **(13,25)**. Read across the row, wrap from **(13,27)** to **(14,1)**, and finish at **(14,4)**. The sequence `L–AE–N–TH–P–U–X` yields `A–S–I–G–O–TH–E`, or **AS I GO THE**.
