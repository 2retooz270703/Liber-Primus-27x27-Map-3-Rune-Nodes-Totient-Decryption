# Strongest Plaintext Evidence

*Starting coordinates → Numerical patterns → Ciphertext checks*

These connections were found after reconstructing the candidate plaintext. Sums use rune indices 0–28 unless stated otherwise. Each transliteration, including TH, EA and NG, counts as one rune.

## 1. The starting J

The red heading on page 0 has 13 runes in two words (8 and 5). The number 13 and its totient, φ(13) = 12, lead to three related calculations:

| Connection | Calculation | Result |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Coordinates | (13, φ(13)) = (13, 12) | J(13,12) |
| Radius | 13 − 12 = 1 | 1 cell |
| Outer value | 13 + 12 = 25 | AE = 25 |

At radius 1, the horizontal cells are:

| Left (13,11) | Center (13,12) | Right (13,13) |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| AE | J | EA |

AE = 25 and EA = 28 are different runes; only their Latin labels are reversed. Starting from the left AE:

φ(J = 11) + φ(10) = 10 + 4 = 14

AE(13,11) → RIGHT 14 → L(13,25)

There is also a possible word-map connection:

158 words − 2 heading words = 156 = 13 × 12

This suggests a 13 × 12 arrangement of the body words. Both starting-point connections were noticed retrospectively; neither yet establishes a selection rule.

## 3. Eight wraps in both blocks

During decryption, a *wrap* occurs when ciphertext minus key is negative and 29 is added. Across an entire block:

Plaintext sum = Ciphertext sum − Key sum + 29 × wraps

| Seven-word block | Ciphertext sum | Key sum | Wraps | Plaintext sum |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| First | 299 | 298 | 8 | 233 |
| Second | 256 | 256 | 8 | 232 |

First: 299 − 298 + 8 × 29 = 233

Second: 256 − 256 + 8 × 29 = 232

Both have 8 wraps. Their ciphertext-minus-key differences are 1 and 0, accounting for the one-point difference between 233 and 232. Also, φ(8) = 4.

## 4. The total through DEATH points to YOU

This section uses prime-valued Gematria Primus, rather than rune indices.

| Plaintext block | Prime-value sum |

2163 = 103 × 7 × 3

| Factor | 103 | 7 | 3 |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Rune | Y | O | U |

The next recovered words are SEE YOU. Reading the prime factors from largest to smallest spells YOU; multiplication itself does not determine their order.

*Seek and you will be found.*

## 6. The two cubes

| Plaintext | Words | Runes | Index sum |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|
| THE IDEA OF THE END IS DEATH | 7 | 17 | 232 |
| SEE YOU SOON | 3 | 10 | 111 |
| Combined | **10** | **27** | **343** |

17 + 10 = 27 = 3³ — 3 words in SEE YOU SOON.

232 + 111 = 343 = 7³ — 7 words in the DEATH block.

The SEE YOU SOON sum is 51 + 30 + 30 = 111. This grouping ends at SOON, before THEN.

These are reproducible numerical correspondences found after the candidate words were reconstructed, not proof of Cicada's intended reading path.
