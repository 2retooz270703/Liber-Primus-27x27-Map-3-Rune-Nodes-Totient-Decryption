# Strongest Plaintext Evidence

*Starting point → Block totals → Numerical connections*

Unless noted otherwise, sums use rune indices (0–28), not Gematria Primus prime values. `TH`, `EA`, and `NG` each count as one rune.

## 1. The starting J

The red heading on page 0 contains **13 runes** in two words (8 + 5). Since φ(13) = 12:

| From 13 and 12 | Calculation | Result |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Position | (13, φ(13)) = (13, 12) | **J(13,12)** |
| Distance | 13 − 12 = 1 | Radius 1 |
| Rune value | 13 + 12 = 25 | AE = 25 |

At radius 1, the horizontal triple is:

| Left (13,11) | Center (13,12) | Right (13,13) |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| AE | **J** | EA |

`AE` and `EA` have reversed Latin labels, but are different runes. Starting from the left `AE`:

**φ(11) + φ(10) = 10 + 4 = 14**

**AE(13,11) → RIGHT 14 → L(13,25)**

The heading's two words also leave **158 − 2 = 156 = 13 × 12** body words — a possible 13 × 12 word arrangement, not yet a reading rule.

## 3. Eight modular wraps

A *wrap* adds 29 when ciphertext minus key is negative.

**Plaintext sum = Ciphertext sum − Key sum + 29 × wraps**

| Block | Ciphertext | Key | Wraps | Plaintext calculation |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|:---|
| First | 299 | 298 | 8 | 299 − 298 + 8 × 29 = **233** |
| Second | 256 | 256 | 8 | 256 − 256 + 8 × 29 = **232** |

Both blocks have **8 wraps** (φ(8) = 4). Their ciphertext-minus-key totals differ by exactly 1.

| Plaintext | Words | Runes | Index sum |
