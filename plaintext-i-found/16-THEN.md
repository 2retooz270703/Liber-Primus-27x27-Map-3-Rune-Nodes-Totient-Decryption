# 16 — THEN

AS I GO, THE WEATHER TURNS COLD. I MAY CRY NOW. THE IDEA OF THE END IS DEATH. SEE YOU SOON, THEN.

## 1. Start from the end of SOON

The primary `SOON` route from the previous chapter, [`15-SOON.md`](./15-SOON.md), ends at **H(25,19)**. Its key established **Möbius phase 2**, which we carry forward into `THEN`.

Apply phase 2 to the endpoint coordinates:

```text
φ²(25) = 8   → μ(8) = 0
φ²(19) = 6   → μ(6) = +1

T₂(25,19) = (8,6)
V₂(25,19) = (0,+1) → RIGHT
```

The selector gives **RIGHT** and retains the values **8** and **6**. Looking along that direction reveals a precise relationship between both numbers and the next ciphertext.

## 2. The numbers point to both ends of the ciphertext

Starting at `H(25,19)`, both distances land on row 25:

- **RIGHT 6** reaches `L(25,25)`.
- **RIGHT 8** reaches `W(25,27)`.

The three consecutive runes between those two positions are:

```text
L(25,25) → T(25,26) → W(25,27)
```

This identifies **ciphertext `L-T-W`**. In the full grid, this exact directed straight three-rune sequence occurs only once.

Both values from the previous phase match the same segment exactly: **6** reaches its beginning, while **8** reaches its end.

## 3. A mirror exactly halfway to the ciphertext

The distance from `H(25,19)` to `L(25,25)` is six cells. Its midpoint is therefore `IA(25,22)`, three cells from either end:

```text
H(25,19) ── 3 ── IA(25,22) ── 3 ── L(25,25)
                       |
                   IA(26,22)  ← center
                       |
                   IA(27,22)
```

Those three vertical runes form **IA-IA-IA** — the only standard mirror of this exact type in the matrix. Because its upper rune lies exactly at the midpoint, this mirror provides a natural source for the key.

Transform the center with Euler's totient:

```text
IA = 27
φ(27) = 18 = E

IA-IA-IA → IA-E-IA
```

Now calculate the key's Möbius phase:

```text
Totient signature: (18,6,18)
Möbius signature: (0,+1,0)
Phase:              1

IA-E-IA → E-IA-IA
```

The resulting **active key is `E-IA-IA`**. Phase **2** located the ciphertext; phase **1** is calculated separately to rotate this new key.

## 4. Decrypting L-T-W

Subtract the generated key from the ciphertext, modulo 29:

| Ciphertext | Key | Calculation | Plaintext |
|---|---|---|---|
| `L = 20` | `E = 18` | `20 − 18 = 2` | **TH** |
| `T = 16` | `IA = 27` | `16 − 27 ≡ 18` | **E** |
| `W = 7` | `IA = 27` | `7 − 27 ≡ 9` | **N** |

```text
Ciphertext: L  - T  - W
Key:        E  - IA - IA
Plaintext:  TH - E  - N
```

The result is **THEN**. It is written with four Latin letters but consists of **three runes**, because `TH` is a single rune.

## 5. THEN connects to the hidden SOON route

The last ciphertext rune of `THEN` is **W(25,27)**. In [`15-SOON.md`](./15-SOON.md), the **secondary, hidden reconstruction of SOON** ends at **W(27,27)**.

These endpoints sit on opposite sides of `NG(26,27)`:

```text
W(25,27)   ← end of THEN
   |
NG(26,27)  ← center
   |
W(27,27)   ← end of secondary SOON
```

Together they form the mirror **W-NG-W**. Its importance is clear from the geometry: it directly joins the end of `THEN` to the endpoint of the previously discovered hidden `SOON` route. This gives a natural reason to use this particular mirror for the next calculation.

Its center produces another phase:

```text
NG = 21 → φ(21) = 12 = EO
W-NG-W → W-EO-W

Totient signature: (6,4,6)
Möbius signature: (+1,0,+1)
Phase:              2
```

Applying the phase generated by this connecting mirror to the end of `THEN` gives:

```text
T₂(25,27) = (8,6)
V₂(25,27) = (0,+1) → RIGHT
```

This reproduces **exactly the same state** found after primary `SOON`: phase **2**, totient pair **(8,6)**, and Möbius pair **(0,+1)**. The endpoints are also **8 columns apart**, matching the first retained value.

The **W-NG-W** connection is especially meaningful because its two outer runes are endpoints of two different routes. It links the hidden `SOON` branch to `THEN` and restores the same mathematical state with which this chapter began.

## 6. One more numerical connection

The rune-index totals of the three preceding words add up to:

```text
SEE + YOU + SOON = 51 + 30 + 30 = 111
φ(111) = 72
```

The generated key for `THEN` has exactly the same sum:

```text
E + IA + IA = 18 + 27 + 27 = 72
```

The sum of the preceding plaintext matches the sum of the new key **exactly: 72 = 72**.

## 7. Conclusion

The continuation **SOON → THEN** brings several parts of the matrix together. The phase inherited from `SOON` points RIGHT, and its two retained values **6** and **8** land precisely on the beginning and end of `L-T-W`. At the midpoint, the unique **IA-IA-IA** mirror generates the key **E-IA-IA**, which decrypts the sequence to **TH-E-N**.

The last rune of `THEN` then connects through **W-NG-W** to the hidden secondary `SOON` route. That mirror regenerates phase **2** and returns the exact coordinate state **(8,6)** with Möbius signature **(0,+1)**. Together, these relationships make `THEN` a tightly connected continuation of the preceding reconstruction.
