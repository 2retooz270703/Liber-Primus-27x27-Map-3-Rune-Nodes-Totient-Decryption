## 1. Locate the nodes

The END ciphertext finishes at I(21,23). Four connected mirrors on row 21 lead to the key and ciphertext:

| Node | Left rune | Center | Right rune | Radius |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| I–R–I | I(21,21) | R(21,22) | I(21,23) | 1 |
| H–R–H | H(21,17) | R(21,22) | H(21,27) | 5 |

H–R–H forms the key. H–L–H and EO–L–EO lead to the ciphertext.

## 3. Read and decrypt

From EO(21,9), read two runes rightward. Subtract the TH–H key modulo 29.

| Cell | Ciphertext | Key | Subtraction (mod 29) | Plaintext |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| (21,9) | EO = 12 | TH = 2 | 12 − 2 ≡ 10 | I |
| (21,10) | D = 23 | H = 8 | 23 − 8 ≡ 15 | S |

The final D(21,10) is the center of E(19,12)–D(21,10)–E(23,8), matching the key's CENTER state (0, +1, 0) in the project's interpretation.
