## About This Research

This repository documents my ongoing research into deciphering **Liber Primus (LP2), pages 0–2** of Cicada 3301.

The approach treats all **729 runes as a single 27×27 matrix**, investigating mathematical relationships between rune positions, three-rune key structures, Euler's totient function, the Möbius function, and Gematria Primus.

This investigation has produced a proposed sequence of English plaintext hidden within the matrix.

### Current Research Status

The proposed plaintext route has revealed multiple interconnected mathematical patterns, some of which reproduce increasingly specific properties of subsequent decryption keys.

**The next major objective is to extend these rules beyond the known route and independently recover new plaintext through prediction rather than retrospective matching.**

This research does not yet constitute a complete, independently verified solution to LP2 pages 0–2. All findings are presented as an open investigation, with documented calculations, supporting evidence, and proposed mechanisms available for independent examination and reproduction.
